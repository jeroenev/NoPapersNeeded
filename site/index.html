Your paperwork, organized. Your files, still yours.

No Papers Needed turns the pile of bills, contracts, receipts and letters into a searchable library on your phone and your computer. Scan a letter, drop in a download, and find it again in seconds by typing a word from the page.

There's no account to create and no server to run. Your documents stay ordinary PDFs in a folder you choose, readable by any other app, today and in ten years.

Plain folder of PDFs

Never locked in a database or proprietary format. Stop using the app and you still have a tidy folder of PDFs.

On-device OCR & search

Why you'll like it

Everything you need to tame everyday paperwork.

Built for both your computer and your phone, without forcing you to maintain a home server or hand your documents to a subscription service.

Find anything

Search the full text of every document, scans included. Text recognition runs in the background, so a photo of a letter becomes searchable too.

Scan in seconds

On Android, point your camera at a page and the scanner crops and straightens it. Share a PDF or photo from any app straight into your library.

Drag, drop, done

On the desktop, drag files into the window or let the app watch an inbox folder and pick up whatever lands there automatically.

Organize your way

Tags (nested if you like), correspondents, document types, dates, notes, and fields you define yourself. Rules can tag new documents automatically based on their text.

Stay on top of new arrivals

A review queue shows what still needs sorting, and saved views keep your favorite filters one tap away.

Every device, one library

Keep the folder in sync with Syncthing or Nextcloud and every phone and computer sees the same documents, tags and search results. Concurrent edits merge cleanly.

Built-in cloud sync

Or mirror the library directly from the app to Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, WebDAV/Nextcloud, S3, Backblaze B2, SFTP and more. Your files stay plain files there too.

Ask your paperwork anything

"When does my car insurance renew?" With an AI provider configured, the assistant searches your library, reads the relevant documents and answers with citations.

Safe by design

Deleted documents go to a trash you can restore from, and the app spots duplicates and files that went missing.

A closer look

Calm, focused interfaces on desktop and phone.

Designed around fast full-text search, side-by-side reading, and structured metadata that stays out of your way until you need it.

On your phone

The same library, laid out for a small screen — with a camera scanner and share-sheet import always in your pocket.

No lock-in

Your folder is the source of truth.

Your files are never converted, renamed behind your back or locked inside a database. Move or rename a PDF in your file manager and its tags, title and notes stay attached.

  • Ordinary PDFs: Open them in any PDF reader on any system, today or ten years from now.
  • Conflict-free sync: Edit tags on your phone while offline and edit fields on your laptop — changes merge cleanly when your folder syncs.
  • Shared OCR: Once one device recognizes the text on a scan, your other devices adopt it instead of redoing the work.

Optional AI assistant

Let the assistant do the filing — on your terms.

Set it up with a provider of your choice using your own API key, or run Ollama on your own machine. There is no account with us and no server of ours in between: requests go from your device straight to that provider.

Works with your provider
Ollama (Local) OpenAI Anthropic Google Gemini Mistral DeepSeek OpenRouter OpenAI-compatible

Ask in plain words

Ask questions across your library; the assistant searches and reads the relevant documents and shows which ones it used. Conversations stay on your device so you can pick them up later.

Bulk edit with review

Have it propose tags, titles, correspondents and dates across the inbox, everything untagged, a saved view or a selection. Every suggestion is shown first before anything is saved.

Read the hard pages

Handwriting, complex tables and faint scans come out as searchable text that regular on-device recognition struggles with.

File as you go

With auto-organize turned on, each new document gets tags from the ones you already use and any field you have marked for the assistant to fill.

Off by default. Bulk edits change nothing until you approve them, and the app warns you before a run sends a large batch, since that is one request per document to your provider.
